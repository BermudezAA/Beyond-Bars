## Confirmed organization information

- **Tagline:** Addressing youth violence through prevention, policy, and systemic change.
- **Mission:** Beyond Bars works to keep young people out of prison by promoting prevention, accountability, and community-based solutions. We center lived experience in conversations about justice and public safety, challenge harmful narratives that normalize incarceration as the default response, and build pathways for youth, families, and communities to thrive beyond the justice system. Through storytelling, education, and advocacy, we seek to influence culture, policy, and practice.

The documented workflow uses Google Sheets for initial data entry and approval; Replit is the intended website and database development platform. The live URL has not been supplied.

## Editing safely

- Keep the YAML front matter at the top of each Markdown page.
- Use the `beyond-bars-site-skeleton` branch for ongoing changes while the pull request is under review.
- Do not publish private personal information, interview transcripts, or identifying details without appropriate authorization and consent.
- Keep the tracker website/database separate from this organization site; only add its public link after the URL is confirmed.
- Check desktop and mobile layouts after changes. Review the latest GitHub Actions checks before merging.

<ul><li><strong>Research and policy:</strong> help locate, verify, and explain legislation and public information.</li><li><strong>Storytelling and writing:</strong> support accessible, respectful communication that centers lived experience.</li><li><strong>Data and visualization:</strong> help organize information and turn findings into clear visual summaries.</li><li><strong>Global youth justice research:</strong> contribute to structured research on access to justice, legal systems, and rights.</li></ul>

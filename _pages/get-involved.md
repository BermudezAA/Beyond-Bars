<p class="bb-eyebrow">GET INVOLVED</p>
<h1>Bring your skills to work that matters.</h1>
<p class="bb-lede">Beyond Bars brings together volunteers working in more than 10 countries to address youth violence through prevention, policy, and systemic change.</p>
<p>Potential volunteers, partners, researchers, and supporters can explore our projects and learn how the work is organized. The organization’s work spans storytelling, legislative tracking, data visualization, and youth-led research into access to justice.</p>
<h2>Ways to contribute</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Research and policy:</strong> support the collection and organization of public legislative information.</li>
  <li><strong>Storytelling and writing:</strong> help communicate lived experiences and knowledge with care.</li>
  <li><strong>Data and visualization:</strong> help aggregate, analyze, map, and communicate data.</li>
  <li><strong>Youth justice research:</strong> contribute to research about young people's access to justice, legal systems, and rights.</li>
</ul>

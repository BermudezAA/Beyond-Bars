<p>The Beyond Bars Digital Policy Tracker monitors legislative developments related to prisons, systemic violence, and youth justice. By organizing information about bills, sponsors, legislative status, and potential implications, the project aims to make public policy easier to follow and give people a clearer view of decisions that can affect their communities.</p>
<h2>How the work is organized</h2>
<p>The working tracker is maintained in Google Sheets as an initial tool for collecting, organizing, and reviewing legislative information. Replit has been identified as the intended platform for future website and database development. The tracker remains a separate initiative from this main Beyond Bars organization website.</p>

<p class="bb-eyebrow">ABOUT BEYOND BARS</p>
<h1>Justice should create pathways forward.</h1>
<p class="bb-lede">Beyond Bars addresses youth violence through prevention, policy, and systemic change.</p>
<p>We work to keep young people out of prison by advancing prevention, accountability, and community-based solutions. We center the lived experience of people affected by youth violence and the justice system, and challenge narratives that treat incarceration as the default response.</p>
<h2>What guides our work</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Center lived experience.</strong> People closest to the issues bring essential knowledge to conversations about justice and public safety.</li><li><strong>Make information accessible.</strong> Storytelling, education, research, and visual communication help communities understand systems and policy.</li><li><strong>Work toward systemic change.</strong> We seek to influence culture, policy, and practice while building pathways for young people, families, and communities to thrive.</li></ul>
<h2>A global volunteer community</h2>
<p>Our volunteers work across 10+ countries, contributing diverse perspectives and skills to a shared commitment to youth justice.</p>

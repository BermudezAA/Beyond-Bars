<p class="bb-eyebrow">PROJECT 04 · GLOBAL RESEARCH</p><h1>Youth Justice Index</h1>
<p class="bb-lede">Compare how young people access justice, legal systems, and their rights around the world.</p>
<p>The Youth Justice Index (YJI) is a youth-led global data collection effort designed to measure key dimensions of young people’s access to justice and legal protections. Its intended outputs include country-level comparisons based on defined indicators.</p>
<h2>Research framework</h2><ul><li>Identify specific, consistently defined youth justice metrics.</li><li>Collect and document comparable country-level information.</li><li>Make findings understandable through transparent methods and clear presentation.</li></ul>

<p class="bb-eyebrow">PROJECT 04 · GLOBAL RESEARCH</p>
<p class="bb-lede">Assess young people's access to justice, legal systems, and rights worldwide.</p>
<p>The Beyond Bars Youth Justice Index is a youth-led data collection initiative designed to assess access to justice, legal systems, and rights for young people worldwide. Through country-level metrics and comparative rankings, it aims to encourage tangible action in response to measurable gaps in youth justice.</p>
<p class="bb-note">The approved indicator framework, country coverage, source list, methodology, ranking status, and publication schedule have not yet been supplied.</p>

<h1>Youth Justice Index</h1>
<p class="bb-lede">Assess young people's access to justice, legal systems, and rights worldwide.</p>
<p>The Beyond Bars Youth Justice Index is a youth-led data collection initiative designed to assess youth justice outcomes across countries. It brings together measurable indicators spanning detention, policing, legal representation, sentencing, conditions in custody, reentry, prevention, and opportunity.</p>

<h2>How the index is structured</h2>
<p>Each country receives a score from 0 to 100 in each of the seven categories below. The indicators listed under each category are used to evaluate available country-level data. Where reported data are available, measures such as youth detention, recidivism, and youth poverty are assessed and converted into a category score. Higher category scores represent stronger youth justice outcomes.</p>

<h2>Indicator framework</h2>

<h3>1. Youth Incarceration &amp; Detention</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Youth incarceration/detention rate per 100,000 young people</li>
  <li>Total number of youth in detention</li>
  <li>Percentage of detained youth who are unsentenced/pretrial</li>
  <li>Average length of youth detention</li>
  <li>Percentage of youth held in adult correctional facilities</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Policing &amp; Youth Arrest</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Youth arrest rate per 100,000 young people</li>
  <li>Number/rate of youth arrested for violent offenses</li>
  <li>Number/rate of youth arrested for nonviolent offenses</li>
  <li>Percentage of youth diverted from formal prosecution</li>
  <li>Rate of youth arrests resulting in detention</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. Courts &amp; Legal Representation</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Percentage of youth with access to legal counsel</li>
  <li>Percentage of youth receiving publicly funded legal representation</li>
  <li>Percentage of youth transferred/tried in adult courts</li>
  <li>Average time from arrest/referral to case disposition</li>
  <li>Percentage of juvenile cases resulting in a formal conviction/adjudication</li>
</ul>

<h3>4. Sentencing &amp; Alternatives</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Percentage of youth receiving custodial sentences</li>
  <li>Percentage receiving probation/community supervision</li>
  <li>Average length of youth sentences</li>
  <li>Percentage of cases resolved through diversion</li>
  <li>Percentage of youth participating in restorative-justice programs</li>
</ul>

<h3>5. Conditions &amp; Rights in Custody</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Percentage of detained youth receiving formal education</li>
  <li>Percentage of detained youth receiving mental-health services</li>
  <li>Percentage of detained youth receiving healthcare</li>
  <li>Average number of family visits/contacts per detained youth</li>
  <li>Number/rate of reported incidents of violence or abuse in youth detention</li>
  <li>Percentage of youth with access to rehabilitation or vocational programs</li>
</ul>

<h3>6. Reentry &amp; Long-Term Outcomes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Youth recidivism/reoffending rate</li>
  <li>Percentage of released youth enrolled in education</li>
  <li>Percentage of released youth employed or participating in vocational training</li>
  <li>Percentage of released youth receiving reentry/support services</li>
</ul>

<h3>7. Prevention, Equity &amp; Opportunity</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Youth poverty rate</li>
  <li>Youth unemployment rate</li>
  <li>School enrollment/attendance rate</li>
  <li>School dropout rate</li>
  <li>Youth mental-health issues rate</li>
  <li>Youth substance-use rate</li>
</ul>

<h2>Scoring and overall result</h2>
<p>Available indicator data are analyzed and converted into a score from 0 to 100 for each category. The direction of each indicator is considered so that higher category scores consistently reflect stronger youth justice outcomes. The seven category scores are then averaged equally to produce the final Youth Justice Index score for each country.</p>
<p><strong>Final Score = (Category 1 + Category 2 + Category 3 + Category 4 + Category 5 + Category 6 + Category 7) ÷ 7</strong></p>
<p class="bb-note">The full methodology will document data sources, country coverage, indicator-level scoring and weighting, and the treatment of unavailable data. The index framework describes what will be measured; it does not imply that country scores or rankings have already been calculated.</p>

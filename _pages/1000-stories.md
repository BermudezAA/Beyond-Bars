<p>The Beyond Bars 1000 Stories Project has a clear goal: collect 1,000 stories from people affected by youth violence and the justice system, as well as people who work alongside young people, families, and communities. Statistics can show the scale of a problem. Stories help us understand the people, circumstances, and experiences behind those numbers.</p>
<p>There is no perfect story, and there is no single experience that defines this issue. A story does not need to be extraordinary, polished, or complete to matter. The project is intended to make room for honest perspectives, including experiences that are often overlooked or left out of public conversations.</p>

<h2>Who can contribute</h2>
<p>The project is intended to welcome perspectives from across the youth justice landscape, including:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Young people and adults with lived experience of youth violence or the justice system.</li>
  <li>People who have experienced incarceration or detention, and their families and loved ones.</li>
  <li>Parents, guardians, caregivers, and members of affected communities.</li>
  <li>Practitioners, educators, researchers, advocates, and others whose work involves youth, safety, or justice.</li>
</ul>
<p>You do not need a particular title or background to have a perspective worth hearing.</p>

<h2>Ways to share a story</h2>
<p>The project is being developed to accommodate different ways of telling a story. Contributions may take the form of written accounts, interviews, audio, video, artwork, or other forms of expression. There are no required prompts or interview questions at this stage. Contributors will be able to communicate in a format that feels appropriate to their experience and comfort.</p>

<h2>Privacy, consent, and care</h2>
<p>Contributors may choose to remain anonymous. Stories about youth violence and the justice system can involve deeply personal experiences, and they should be handled with care. Beyond Bars is developing the participation and consent process, including clear information about how identifying details and stories may be collected, used, and shared.</p>
<p>No story will be published on this website without the contributor's permission. The project will explain its privacy and consent procedures, including additional safeguards for contributors under 18, before it begins accepting submissions.</p>

<h2>Working toward 1,000 stories</h2>
<p>The submission form and participation process are still in development. Submissions are not yet open, and no stories have been published here. This page will be updated with instructions and relevant safeguards once the process is ready.</p>
<p>For questions about the project, contact <a href="mailto:info.beyondbars@gmail.com">info.beyondbars@gmail.com</a>.</p>
